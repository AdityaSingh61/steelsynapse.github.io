Our Big Idea
Flipped the Script
"Instead of adding attachments to the robot, the robot gets added to the attachments."
We completely rethought how Tower interacts with its arms. By docking the robot into pre-positioned attachments instead of swapping arms onto the robot, we unlocked speed, precision, and creativity.
Quicker turnaround between missions
More personalised arms per task
Less room for error
