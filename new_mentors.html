Our Mentors

Each season, we have been blessed with mentors who guided us regularly on different aspects of our FLL journey. Our coaches were always present, motivating us through every challenge. We want to acknowledge their incredible contribution to our team.

FLL Coordinator

The one who is always there for us

Mr. Mathew (Matt) Wilson

Mr. Wilson began his robotics career in 2016 with Robomatter, creating curricula for VEX and LEGO platforms and serving as a programming judge at VEX Worlds. He later mentored winning competitive teams, including the Gear Grinders, and has since expanded USC Robotics by guiding teams to state championships, running workshops, and supporting coaches locally and nationally. Now, as USC FLL Coordinator, he is committed to building a strong and thriving robotics community.

Season Coaches

Our Current Coaches

Ms. Meenakshi Sundaram

Meenakshi (Minnie) Sundaram is a physical therapist working in research at the University of Pittsburgh. As a parent of two USCSD students, she has coached middle school robotics for the past two years and actively volunteers at Streams. She is passionate about allowing creativity, problem-solving, technical, and teamwork skills to flourish.

Dr. Aditi Gurkar

Dr. Gurkar is a Professor at the University of Pittsburgh and has coached FLL for the past two years. She loves to connect us to our community and inspire us to make a difference. Her emphasis for us is to learn and work together but also independently.

Dr. Slava Rovner

This is his third season coaching a middle school FLL team. He has a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering and enjoys sharing his passion for programming and engineering with young minds. His goal has been to create a fun and inclusive environment where every team member feels heard.

Past Seasons

Additional Mentors

Ms. Rachel Roos

Ms. Roos is a bioengineer who worked at the University of Pittsburgh. In the 2023 season she guided us through our innovation project — creating an augmented reality LEGO building experience — by providing feedback on our designs and guiding us in the use of materials to create our sensors.

Mr. Zakiy Alfikri

Mr. Alfikri is a bioengineer working at the University of Pittsburgh. In the 2023 season he along with Ms. Roos guided us through our innovation project, especially helping us understand the underlying principles of the proposed solution.

Mr. Sainyam Arora

Mr. Arora is a chemical engineer working for Jameson Mathey in Savannah. In the 2024 season he guided us through our prototype based on battery principles for ocean water electrolysis to combat ocean acidification, prodding us to think about the feasibility of our proposed solution.

Ms. Samiksha Kature

Ms. Kature is a software engineer working as a data analyst at Jameson Mathey in Philadelphia. She volunteered her time in the 2025 season to train us on coding using the Python language.
