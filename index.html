Who We Are

Forged in Steel.
Connected by Code.

Steel Synapse is a group of enthusiastic students exploring science, technology, engineering, and math through the First Lego League program.

Seven members, unique skills, and one shared drive — guided by great coaches to build robots, solve real-world problems, and grow together every season.
