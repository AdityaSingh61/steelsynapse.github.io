Our History

From our first robot to our latest season — click each year to explore our journey.

Our 1st year we were a team of 6 members (Genevieve, Naisha, Prachi, Rayansh, Siddh and Tarun). Our mentors were Mr. Mike Ressler and Ms. Minnie Sundaram. This season was a huge learning curve for us — from understanding the rules of FLL to learning Scratch programming for coding, as well as learning to work as a team. It was a great season as we learnt so much and won 3rd place in Core Values during the regional competition.

Benny's old team was the Brick Busters (#53462).

We were reassigned into teams and only 3 of us from the original team ended up together, along with 2 new members (Ayan, Benny, Rayansh, Siddh and Tarun). We were mentored by Mr. Slava Rovner and Ms. Minnie Sundaram. This was a season where we were more organized and used different strategies. The season was great in terms of learning engineering design principles and creating prototypes. We won twice in the scrimmages and had a special recognition for our Robot design.

Adi and Porter's old team was the Robo Birds.

This season we have been lucky as we were able to retain all members of the team and add 2 new members, making us the Steely 7 (Adi, Ayan, Benny, Porter, Rayansh, Siddh and Tarun). This season we have 3 mentors in Mr. Slava Rovner, Dr. Aditi Gurkar and Ms. Minnie Sundaram. We plan on continuing with this name and identity to Forge New Solutions to the challenges we face together.
