Steel Synapse: FLL Team #53463

About Us

Steel Synapse is a group of enthusiastic students who love exploring science, technology, engineering and math (STEM) through the First Lego League (FLL) program.

Our team is made up of seven members, each with unique skills and ideas eager to collaborate, learn, and work towards improving ourselves. Motivated and guided by our coaches, we enjoy problem solving, designing robots, and working with each other.

We believe teamwork and having fun are the important foundations to achieving our goals. Our mission is to grow as problem solvers, inventors and friends while making a positive impact through STEM.
